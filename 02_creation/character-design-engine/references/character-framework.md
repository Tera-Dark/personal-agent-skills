# Character Framework

A strong character design contains:

## Identity

Who is this character?

Examples:

- forgotten princess
- wandering swordswoman
- moon priestess

## Visual Hook

The memorable element:

- silhouette
- hairstyle
- iconic costume feature
- unique motif

## Costume Language

Define:

- culture
- era
- social status
- materials
- craftsmanship

## Personality Expression

Shown through:

- pose
- expression
- gesture
- styling choices

## Avoid

Do not create:

"beautiful girl with random outfit"

Create:

"a character with a visual identity and story implication"
