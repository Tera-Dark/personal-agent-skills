# OC Maximalist Close-Up Backdrop + Element Sticker Preset

> A reusable composition preset for original-character presentation. This defines the **layout grammar**, not a mandatory art style or a list of decorative objects.

## Core structure

**Back layer → oversized close-up → main full-body figure → floating headshots / element stickers → selected foreground overlaps.**

The oversized close-up is the **compositional base**. It is not a small inset and not a decorative portrait placed beside the character.

### Required hierarchy

1. **Oversized close-up backdrop**
   - Same character's face / bust enlarged dramatically.
   - Fills a large portion of the rear canvas.
   - Cropped by the frame.
   - Faded, softened, or semi-transparent enough to sit behind the foreground.
   - Establishes the dominant background silhouette and color mass.

2. **Main full-body figure**
   - Sits clearly in the foreground and overlaps the enlarged face.
   - Remains the primary readable character.
   - May cross directly over facial features, hair, shoulders, or clothing of the backdrop.
   - Should have a designed pose rather than a neutral standing pose.

3. **Floating headshots / element stickers**
   - Several smaller bust portraits or headshot crops of the same character.
   - Distributed around the main figure rather than placed in a regular grid.
   - Can overlap the main figure, backdrop portrait, ribbons, ornaments, or each other.
   - May include themed mini mascots, charms, accessory crops, flowers, props, or costume-detail fragments when they reinforce the character.

4. **Foreground graphic fragments**
   - Cropped hands, accessories, ribbons, flowers, fabric, props, borders, or small decorative illustrations may overlap the lower or side edges.
   - Use as compositional connectors, not filler.

## Visual rule

The key visual read should happen as:

**giant face in the back → full-body character in front → small headshots / stickers around the figure.**

The viewer should understand the depth hierarchy immediately, even at thumbnail size.

## Composition behavior

- Strong vertical or portrait-oriented layout is preferred.
- Use aggressive scale contrast: giant face / full body / small portraits / tiny motifs.
- Use partial cropping and overlap to make the layers feel physically stacked.
- Favor asymmetrical distribution rather than a neat character-sheet grid.
- Keep some clean or pale background visible so the dense collage has breathing room.
- The backdrop portrait should remain visually recognizable but subordinate to the full-body figure.
- Small headshots should read as intentional fragments of the same character presentation, not unrelated duplicates.

## Style behavior

Suitable for:
- original-character showcase
- female-oriented social-media character art
- maximalist fashion illustration
- collectible-character presentation
- decorative scrapbook / sticker collage
- ornate OC commission showcase

This preset can be combined with lace, pearls, flowers, ribbons, feathers, translucent fabric, gothic, Victorian, Chinese-inspired, fantasy, confectionery, animal-hybrid, or other character-specific styling. The **composition preset stays fixed while the character language changes**.

## Anti-patterns

Do not:
- place the close-up as a small corner portrait;
- create a regular 2x2 character-sheet grid;
- treat the close-up as an ordinary background illustration;
- replace the main full-body figure with another bust portrait;
- distribute stickers evenly around all four sides;
- add decorative objects without reinforcing scale, overlap, or character identity.
