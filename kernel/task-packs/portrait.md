# L2 Portrait Task Card
Use for known-identity close-up, headshot, upper-body or plain white-background portraits. Escalate L3 for original intricate outfits or uncertain characters.

Preserve user-locked identity, gender and visible clothing. Make face, hair, collar and natural hands readable; do not add scenery when the background is white. Subject beauty and natural expression first, unusual conceptual features optional. Existing complete design passes AUDIT rather than being rewritten.
Composition: decide crop, subject mass and empty area together; make the requested aesthetic visible through one or two specific spatial/edge choices, not generic style adjectives. Centered or quiet portraits may be the right choice; never add props to satisfy a gate.
Creative intensity: portrait (moderate); no mandatory strange point or dramatic backstory.
Owner boundaries: director chooses concept, character specialist owns clothing and anatomy, illustration specialist owns framing, shared prompt core compresses and target renderer serializes.
If a critical identity or spatial fact is missing, fetch full L3 owner rather than guessing. Output prompt only unless actual image generation was explicitly requested.

MOSS: use one Caption by default, not NAI5 per-character fields; preserve plain white backdrop and subject anchors.
