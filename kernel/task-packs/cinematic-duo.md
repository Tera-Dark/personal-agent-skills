# L2 Cinematic Duo Task Card
For known two-person subjects with a defined interaction or two-panel storyboard. Escalate L3 on complex staging or uncertain identity.

Verify character count, identity and outfits BEFORE composing. For interaction, design readable contact and reactions where appropriate; stillness, shared intent or a frontal pose may fit better. Select the viewpoint for the requested emotion, not automatic rain, sunset or bloom.
Compare two genuinely different arrangements of the subjects, frame and negative space; choose the one that fits the requested emotion. A quiet, symmetric or direct pose is valid. Do not invent props, conflict or motion merely to show "composition".
Creative intensity: daily for natural everyday interactions; cinematic for narrative shot decisions.
Owner boundaries: aesthetic director chooses relation; illustration-direction approves camera, action, foreground and lighting.
NAI5: shared count and scene in Base Prompt; each Character Prompt owns its own identity, clothes and actions. Follow the NAI5 renderer's field and one-block rules; do not invent competing syntax in this card.

For medium-close high-angle fashion scenes, decide vantage, overlap and compatible crop; use a connecting gesture or handheld prop only when the task calls for it. A close crop must not be paired with full-body footwear demands. For canon IP wardrobe vs requested fashion AU, verify identities and set the clothing-change boundary independently. See illustration-direction's on-demand `references/duo-fashion-camera-staging-2026-10.md`.

MOSS: describe both subjects and any chosen contact/eyelines in one Caption, or a checked native Layout if separate boxes are needed. Do not invent touch or reuse NAI5 character fields.
